# Topic:                                  Newton’s Gravitational Equation: 

F = G × m₁ × m₂ ÷ r²

Date: July 22, 2026
Notes: Force  
The total pulling power between two objects.
Higher force = stronger attraction.                                 Gravitational Constant (G)  
A fixed number that sets the overall strength of gravity in the universe. It never changes.                                    Mass One × Mass Two  
The masses of both objects multiplied together.
Bigger masses = stronger gravity                                     Distance Squared (r²)  
The space between the objects, multiplied by itself.
More distance = much weaker gravity (because it’s squared).
